### Breaking Changes:

- **Requires the 2.0 bridge and embedded driver**: `wdio-dioxus` 2.0 depends on `wdio-dioxus-bridge` 2.x and `wdio-dioxus-embedded-driver` 2.x, and works with `@wdio/dioxus-service` 2.0. Set `wdio-dioxus = "2"` and update the service with it. See [Update the crates together](https://github.com/webdriverio/desktop-mobile/blob/wdio-dioxus-service@v2.0.0/packages/dioxus-service/docs/migration/v1-to-v2.md#update-the-crates-together)
- **`on_window` turns on focus emulation**: With the embedded driver on Windows, `install()` and `on_window` turn on WebView2's focus emulation, so focus moves fire `focus` and `blur` in a background window, and the page stays focused and visible. An app with its own `with_on_window` callback must call `wdio_dioxus::on_window` from it to get this. See [Apps with their own window callback](https://github.com/webdriverio/desktop-mobile/blob/wdio-dioxus-service@v2.0.0/packages/dioxus-service/docs/migration/v1-to-v2.md#apps-with-their-own-window-callback) ([#965](https://github.com/webdriverio/desktop-mobile/pull/965))
